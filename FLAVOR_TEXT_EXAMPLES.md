# 🎴 Real Flavor Text Examples

## The Problem: Generic AI Text

When you ask AI generically for flavor text, you get boring descriptions:

### ❌ BEFORE (Generic Prompts):

**Dragon Card:**
> "A powerful dragon that breathes fire and dominates the battlefield with ancient strength."

**Undead Card:**
> "This ancient undead creature rises from the grave with immense power."

**Sea Monster Card:**
> "A legendary sea creature that terrorizes sailors with its might."

**See the problem?** They all sound the same: "[Thing] with [power word]."

---

## The Solution: Dynamic Style System

Now each card randomly gets one of 10 writing styles with specific instructions:

### ✅ AFTER (Dynamic System):

#### Dragon Card - Prophecy Style:
> "When the sky weeps ash and the mountains crack like eggs, the First Fire will wake."

#### Dragon Card - Survivor's Tale:
> "They say it moves like lightning strikes twice. I only saw it once. Once was all I needed."

#### Dragon Card - Sensory Horror:
> "First comes the smell of burning stone. Then comes the screaming."

#### Undead Card - Ancient Text:
> "The scrolls warn: those who disturb the deep tombs wake not the dead, but Death itself."

#### Undead Card - Last Words:
> "The cold wasn't what killed me. It was realizing the cold had a name."

#### Sea Monster Card - Domain:
> "Ships avoid the Seventh Deep not for the storms, but for what rises when the water goes still."

#### Sea Monster Card - Moment Before:
> "The ocean surface shattered like glass. Then everything went dark."

## Style-by-Style Examples

### 1. Prophecy Style
Cryptic, ancient, mystical language:
- "In the depths where light fears to tread, even the bravest whisper prayers to forgotten gods."
- "The old texts speak of three signs: blood moon, silent birds, and the Watcher's return."
- "When the stars align and the bones sing, that which was banished will walk again."

### 2. Battle Moment Style
Visceral, dramatic combat:
- "It took seventeen warriors to bring it down. None lived to claim glory."
- "The battlefield fell silent. Then came the screaming."
- "They charged with a thousand men. Thirty-seven heartbeats later, none remained standing."

### 3. Survivor's Tale Style
Personal, haunting encounters:
- "I saw it once. Once was enough."
- "They say it moves between heartbeats. I believe them now."
- "We were twelve at dawn. By dusk, I was telling myself I'd always been alone."

### 4. Origin Myth Style
Mystical creation stories:
- "Born from the first nightmare, it crawls through dreams to feed on the waking world."
- "Before the sun learned to shine, before death had a name, it waited in the spaces between stars."
- "They say the gods created it as a test. The gods are no longer worshipped here."

### 5. Last Words Style
Chilling final thoughts:
- "The cold wasn't what killed me. It was realizing the cold had a name."
- "Three things never return from the deep places: mercy, sanity, and those who seek them."
- "My last thought was oddly calm: I should have listened to the silence."

### 6. Sensory Horror Style
Atmospheric dread:
- "Where it walks, the earth remembers pain."
- "First comes the smell of copper. Then comes the understanding."
- "The air tastes of static and ancient things that should not wake."

### 7. Ancient Text Style
Formal, scholarly records:
- "The Grimoire Noctis speaks of one who dwells beyond the edge of maps, waiting for the worthy to find it."
- "According to the Lost Testament: 'And they who seek the Hollow King shall find only the memory of kingdoms.'"
- "The forbidden archives warn of the Eternal Hunger, though the warning itself has been partially devoured."

### 8. Domain Style
Foreboding territories:
- "No birds sing in the hollow. No wind stirs the trees. Nothing leaves alive."
- "The cave entrance beckons. The bones scattered outside do not."
- "They built twelve watchtowers around its lair. Now they build memorials for the watchers."

### 9. Reputation Style
Legend-building among people:
- "Veterans speak its name only in daylight, and never more than once."
- "Hunters tell stories about it to scare apprentices. Survivors tell stories to warn them."
- "In the capital, they debate if it's real. In the borderlands, they debate if running helps."

### 10. Moment Before Style
Building tension:
- "The water went still. The birds stopped singing. Then the world held its breath."
- "In the heartbeat before it struck, everything seemed peaceful. That's what makes the memory worse."
- "The silence broke first. Then everything else followed."

## The Magic Formula

For EVERY card, the system now:

1. ✅ Randomly selects a style
2. ✅ Gives specific instructions for that style
3. ✅ Provides examples of GOOD text
4. ✅ Provides examples of BAD text to avoid
5. ✅ Enforces strict rules (no names, no quotes, no literal descriptions)
6. ✅ Focuses on emotion and atmosphere

Result: **Professional-quality flavor text every time!**
